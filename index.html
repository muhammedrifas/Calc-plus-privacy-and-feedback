Privacy Policy for Calc+

Effective Date: November 2026

1. Data Collection & Usage

Calc+ is designed as an offline utility tool. All computations (including math, unit conversions, financial projections, and date calculations) are performed locally on your device.

We do not collect, transmit, store, or sell any personal information, location data, or calculation history.

2. Permissions

Calc+ requires zero invasive system permissions. No access to contacts, storage, camera, or network sensors is requested.

3. User Feedback & Bug Submissions

If you voluntarily submit bug reports or feedback via our feedback portal, only the information you explicitly provide (description, device model, or optional contact info) is collected solely for debugging and product improvement.

4. Contact

For questions or feature requests, contact us via the feedback option inside the app.
